How It Works in 3 Easy Steps

Simple civic reporting that connects households, local councils, and private haulers.

1

Snap & Pin Location

Take a smartphone photo of the waste pile, enter your parish, and submit your report with no login required.

2

Verify & Dispatch

Your Local Council (LC1) and KCCA health officers verify the incident and assign a collector.

3

Clean & Public Update

Refuse is hauled away and the result is posted to the public resolution log.

Waste Sorting Quick-Start

Sorting at home keeps food scraps and plastics out of drains and landfills:

  • Green: Organic banana peels and kitchen waste (for compost)
  • Blue: Rigid plastic bottles and jerrycans (for recycling)
  • Yellow: Clean polythene bags (kavera)

About Kampala CleanConnect

A student-built portal responding to Greater Kampala's waste problem after the Kiteezi landfill collapse. Built for Victoria University Coursework Project 2.

Demo notice: this site has no live hotline. Listings, numbers and figures are sample data. For real sanitation issues, contact KCCA through kcca.go.ug.
