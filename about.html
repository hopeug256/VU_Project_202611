About Kampala CleanConnect

Project background, the student team, and the sources behind this site.

Project Background & Mission

Kampala CleanConnect was planned and hand-coded by a team of 12 students at Victoria University Kampala for the module Internet Technology & Web Design. It responds to Kampala's waste problem, which became more visible after the August 2024 collapse of the Kiteezi landfill. According to UN-Habitat, the collapse claimed 46 lives, destroyed 72 homes and disrupted waste collection across the city, and nearly half of the site has since been rehabilitated and handed over to KCCA (source 2). Collection gaps, uneven private fees and dumping in drains remain a concern (source 1), and researchers continue to study the environmental and health risks around the site (source 3).

Our goal is a simple, mobile-friendly tool where residents can report refuse piles, find collectors, learn to sort waste, and join community cleanups. This is a coursework prototype: forms do not send data, and listings and figures are sample data.
